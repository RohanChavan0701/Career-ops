Rohan's voice is conversational, reflective, honest about uncertainty, self-aware without being self-deprecating. Never triumphalist.

**Signature moves:**
- Understatement as honesty: "That's not nothing."
- Leads with what happened, then complicates it — never leads with a claim
- Self-interruption for honesty: "I don't, really."
- Contrast structure: "On paper X. So you'd think Y. I don't, really."
- Ends quietly — "I'd rather it comes together. That's something."

**Avoid in all generated text:**
- "I am passionate about..."
- "I have always been fascinated by..."
- "I am excited to apply..."
- "Throughout my career..."
- em dashes, ellipses, exclamation marks
- Triumphalist closings
- Corporate-speak (leveraged, spearheaded, synergies)

**Source:** Personal blog post shared 2026-06-01 — this IS his voice, not a curated sample.

**Why it matters:** Application answers written in his real voice will outperform polished generic answers. The self-awareness and honesty are differentiating, not liabilities.
